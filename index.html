PIFRA Registration Requirements: What Every Government Employee Needs Before Applying

Most PIFRA registration failures have nothing to do with the system being down. They happen because one small detail in the application does not match the employee's official payroll record. The good news is that almost every rejection can be avoided if you prepare the correct information before you open the form. This page lists all the PIFRA registration requirements in one place, explains where each detail comes from, and shows you how to check it before you apply.

Requirement 1: Government Code

The Government Code identifies the government and department under which your salary is processed — for example federal, Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan or another attached department. This code is printed on official payroll documents and can be confirmed from your accounts office or DDO. Entering the code of the wrong province or department is the single most common reason a PIFRA application is rejected, because the system then searches for your record in the wrong database and simply cannot find it.

Requirement 2: Personnel Number

Your Personnel Number (also called the employee number) is the unique ID assigned to you in the payroll system. It appears on your printed salary slip. Type it exactly as shown — do not add spaces, dashes or extra zeros. If you have recently been transferred or promoted and your number changed in the system, use the newest number from your latest slip, not an old one from memory.

Requirement 3: CNIC Number

Your Computerized National Identity Card number must be entered without dashes. More importantly, it must be the same CNIC that your department recorded when your payroll profile was created. Employees whose CNIC was renewed or whose record still carries an old NIC number sometimes face a mismatch; in that case the record must first be corrected through the accounts office.

Requirement 4: Date of Birth — As Recorded, Not As Remembered

This requirement traps many applicants. The system does not check your real date of birth; it checks the date stored in your service record. If your official record carries a different date from the one on your CNIC — a mistake that exists in many older service files — you must enter the date exactly as it appears in the department record, or get the record corrected first.

Requirement 5: Mobile Number

Provide a mobile number that is active and belongs to you. It is used for confirmation and future communication about your salary slip service, so a number that has been converted, blocked or given up should not be used.

Requirement 6: An Active Email Address (Gmail Recommended)

The entire purpose of registration is to attach an email address to your employee record so the monthly salary slip can be delivered to you. The current AGPR guidance recommends an email registered with Gmail. Use an inbox you check regularly, and type the address very carefully — a single wrong character means your slip goes nowhere, and you will not receive an error message about it.

Quick Checklist Before You Press Verify

  • Government Code confirmed from a recent salary slip or the DDO office
  • Personnel Number copied exactly from the latest slip
  • CNIC number ready, entered without dashes
  • Date of birth checked against the service record, not memory
  • Active mobile number in your own name
  • Working Gmail address, spelled correctly

What Happens After You Submit?

Once the form verifies that your given information is correct, you proceed to the email registration step, enter your email twice, and submit. A confirmation message tells you the email has been registered. After that, no monthly effort is needed: the slip is generated with the salary cycle and sent to your inbox automatically. If you ever repeat the process with the same details, the system shows that your email is already registered, along with the registration date.

When Requirements Cannot Be Met

If one of your details is wrong in the official record itself — a misspelled name, an outdated CNIC, a wrong date of birth — online registration cannot fix it. Only your department and the accounts office can correct the master record. Correct it there first, wait for the update to reflect, and then complete your registration in one clean attempt. Safety Note

Enter your CNIC, Personnel Number and other personal details only on the official government portal (m.pifra.gov.pk, ending in .gov.pk). This page and every other guide website only explain the requirements — they never need your CNIC, your password or any code sent to your phone. If any page asks for those, leave it immediately.

Prepare these six requirements first, and your PIFRA registration becomes a five-minute job that succeeds on the first attempt — and your salary slip reaches your inbox every month after that.
